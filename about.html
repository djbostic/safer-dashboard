About this page

Mission · TAP Support · Data Transparency
What this tool does
California's water systems move in and out of SAFER compliance often, but the state doesn't publish a dated, public archive of daily status — only the current snapshot. That makes it hard for water systems and Technical Assistance Providers to prove a system was Failing (or wasn't) on a specific past date for grant eligibility and funding-prioritization purposes. This tool builds that archive, one day at a time, and surfaces it as something you can search, filter, and cite.
SAFER Statuses
What the four statuses mean
Not At-Risk — currently meeting drinking water standards.
Potentially At-Risk — early warning signs the Risk Assessment flags.
At-Risk — meaningful risk of failing without intervention.
Failing — not meeting standards right now (the HR2W Failing list).
Methodology · Automation
How we built this
A script pulls the state's full SAFER risk-assessment data every day, compares it to the day before, and commits any changes — including which specific field moved — to a permanent, dated history. Nothing is edited after the fact, so what you see for a past date is exactly what was recorded that day. Each day's full pull is kept as its own file, so looking up a past month never means downloading the whole history at once.

Caveats · Sources
Data quality & methods
The Failing list updates daily; the broader Risk Assessment (and the indicators behind At-Risk and Potentially At-Risk) refreshes quarterly, so most days show no Risk Assessment movement even when the Failing list does. History before this tool existed was backfilled from an earlier internal export, which recorded status changes but not the specific violation behind each one, and doesn't include the risk-assessment attribute/category detail (or system locations) that snapshots pulled from the live API carry going forward. Source: California State Water Resources Control Board, SAFER dataset, data.ca.gov.
